Privacy Policy

Your browsing stays yours

BrowseWide does not collect, store or share any personal information.

Last updated 24 October 2026.

What stays on your iPhone

Your history, bookmarks, open tabs and settings are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Private tabs keep no history, cookies or website data after you close them.

Websites and search engines

The websites you visit, and the search engine you choose (Google, DuckDuckGo, Bing or Kagi), receive your requests as they would in any browser, and handle them under their own privacy policies. BrowseWide fetches each site’s own icon for the start page directly from that site.

No tracking

BrowseWide contains no analytics, tracking or advertising code, and there are no accounts. Its ad and cookie-banner blocking runs entirely on your device using lists built into the app.

Children

BrowseWide collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
