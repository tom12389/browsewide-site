Support

How can we help?

Found a bug, a site that doesn’t work, or have an idea? Get in touch and we’ll get back to you.

Contact support

Opens a support request on GitHub. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and the website’s address if it’s about a particular site.

Common questions

A website doesn’t work properly

Content blocking can occasionally hide something a site needs. Tap the … button and choose Turn Off Blocking for that site. You can turn it back on the same way, or review every site in Settings › Blocking.

How do I open a private tab?

Touch and hold the tabs button and choose New Private Tab. Private tabs keep no history, cookies or website data.

I closed a tab by mistake

Touch and hold the tabs button and choose Reopen Closed Tab.

How do I bookmark a page?

Touch and hold the book button, or tap … and choose Add Bookmark. Bookmarks appear as tiles on the start page.

Can I change the search engine?

Yes: tap …, then Settings, and pick Google, DuckDuckGo, Bing or Kagi.

Where do downloads go?

Into the Files app, under On My iPhone › BrowseWide › Downloads. Settings › Downloads › Show Downloads in Files takes you straight there.

Can I use the normal status bar on iPhone Duo?

Yes. Settings › iPhone Duo swaps the battery ring around the camera for the system’s own time, battery and signal.

Does BrowseWide work on other iPhones?

Yes. On other iPhones it’s a clean, full-screen browser with all the same features; the camera clearance is just for iPhone Duo.
